## Purpose

Walk a new member into the space so they can work, print, book and get in the door without coming back to the desk five times.

## When to do this

Their first day. Ideally arranged when their contract is countersigned.

## Before they arrive

Check the system side is done, or their first day is spent on admin:

1. **Contract countersigned** — see [Countersign](../contracts/countersign-a-contract.md). Until it is, nothing else has fired.
2. **Portal invite sent and accepted** — see [Invite a member to the portal](../companies-members/invite-a-member-to-the-portal.md). Their set-password link expires in 24 hours, so time it for the day before, not a week ahead.
3. **Door access provisioned** — see [Provision Salto access](../spaces-access/provision-salto-access.md). If we're in ops-task mode, that email needs actioning *before* they arrive.
4. **Space ready** — clean, furnished, keys or fobs to hand.
5. **Directory listing** — they should have had the confirm-your-listing link in the getting-started pack.

## The walkthrough

6. **Their space.** Suite or desk, and what's included.
7. **Access.** How they get in — fob, remote, and the app's **My Key**. Test it with them at the door; don't assume.
8. **Wi-Fi.** Network **Hexa Spaces**, password from Settings.
9. **Printing.** Set them up properly now — see [Set a member up for printing](printer-setup-for-members.md). Show them their PIN in the app and where the machine is.
10. **Kitchen and lounge.** Coffee, tea, filtered water. How the machine works if it's self-serve.
11. **Meeting rooms.** Which are free on their plan, their monthly credits, and how to book — see [How members book rooms](../bookings/how-members-book-rooms.md).
12. **End-of-trip facilities** — showers, bike storage.
13. **Parking** — onsite, plus Trio Box Hill (free underground, Wellington Road entry) and Box Hill Central.
14. **Mail.** Reception receives it; they're emailed when something arrives.
15. **The app.** Get it on their home screen before they leave the desk — see [How to use the member app](using-the-member-app.md).
16. **Who to ask.** Reception for anything; Messages in the app or portal.

## Their address

Give them the exact registered address for ASIC, Google, bank and deliveries:

- **Private office** — `<Suite>, Level <n>/830 Whitehorse Road, Box Hill VIC 3128`
- **Desk or virtual office** — `Level 4/830 Whitehorse Road, Box Hill VIC 3128`

This is already in their getting-started email; confirming it in person prevents a wrong address on their business registration.

## Common mistakes

- **Inducting before the contract is countersigned.** No access, no portal, no invoices — nothing has fired.
- **Sending the portal invite a week early.** It expires in 24 hours.
- **Telling them about printing instead of setting it up.** They'll be back.
- **Skipping the door test.** Access provisioning may be a manual step that nobody actioned.
- **Not getting the app onto their phone.** It's where the door key, print PIN and bookings live.
- **Giving the generic address to a private-office member.** Theirs includes their suite.

## If something goes wrong

- **They can't get in the door** — see [Incidents and lockouts](incidents-and-lockouts.md). Let them in, then fix the access.
- **No portal login** — resend the invite; it's a 24-hour link.
- **The space isn't ready** — be honest, give a time, and put them somewhere workable meanwhile.
