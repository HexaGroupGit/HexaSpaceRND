## Purpose

Leave the space in the state you'd want to find it, and make sure nothing is left running, unsecured or unresolved.

## When to do this

End of the staffed day.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** closing time, weekday vs weekend, and who closes.

## The thing to remember

**Members have 24/7 access.** Closing does not mean locking the floor — people will work through the evening. It means the *staffed* day is ending: reception is unattended, and anything needing a person has to be dealt with now or handed over.

## Steps — hand over the open items

1. **Mail & Deliveries** — anything logged today and uncollected stays logged. Don't clear it.
2. **Messages** — reply to anything outstanding, or note it for tomorrow. Don't leave a member's message read and unanswered overnight.
3. **Maintenance** — update anything worked on today; set it **resolved** only if genuinely fixed.
4. **Food orders** — no order left on **Placed** or **Accepted**. Mark delivered or cancel.
5. Check tomorrow's **Bookings** so the morning has no surprises — early starts, function setups, tours.

## Steps — kitchen and coffee

6. Run the **coffee machine end-of-day routine** — see [Coffee machine — daily](coffee-machine-daily.md). This is the one that actually matters for machine life.
7. Milk away, fridge closed, sink and benches clear, dishwasher run.
8. Bins — kitchen and lounge.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** where bins go, which nights, and any recycling or organics separation.

## Steps — the floor

9. Walk the floor: meeting rooms reset, chairs in, whiteboards wiped, AV cables coiled.
10. Lost property to wherever it lives.
    > **NEEDS INPUT:** where lost property is kept and how long it's held.
11. Turn down or off what should be: lighting in unoccupied zones, AV displays, anything on standby.
    > **NEEDS INPUT:** exactly what gets turned off vs left on for 24/7 members, and any HVAC setback.

## Steps — secure

12. Check external doors close and latch properly.
13. Reception desk clear — nothing sensitive left out. No printouts with member details, no keys, no fobs.
14. Fobs and remotes back in the drawer, and any issued today recorded — see [Issue a fob or remote](../spaces-access/issue-a-fob.md).

> **NEEDS INPUT:** alarm or security procedure, if any. Which doors are checked, whether anything is armed, and who to call if a door won't secure.

## Common mistakes

- **Locking up as though the building empties.** Members will still be here. Don't turn off what they need.
- **Leaving a food order open.** The member sees the status.
- **Clearing uncollected mail.** It stays on their portal until collected.
- **Skipping the coffee machine end-of-day** because it looks clean. That's the routine that prevents the expensive fault.
- **Leaving member details on the desk.** Printouts, notes and screens.

## If something goes wrong

- **A door won't secure** — do not leave it. Escalate that evening.
- **Something breaks at closing** — log it under Maintenance with a note that it happened after hours, so the morning shift sees it before members do.
- **A member is still working and needs something** — help them, then close. The 24/7 members are the ones who notice the space most.
