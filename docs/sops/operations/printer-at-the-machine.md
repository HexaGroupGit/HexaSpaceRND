## Purpose

Keep the printers running and get a member's job out of the queue.

## When to do this

A member can't print, a machine is showing an error, or you're on the morning walk.

## Know which machine

| System | Floors | Member's PIN comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Hexa-Secure** (PaperCut) | Levels 4 & 5 | Portal / app → Printing |
| **Canon / uniFlow** | Level 2 | Emailed separately — a different PIN |

A member who says "my PIN doesn't work" is very often standing at the other system's machine.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** make and model of each machine, and where each one physically is.

## Releasing a job

1. Member enters their **print PIN** at the machine keypad.
2. Their held jobs list; they select and release.
3. If nothing lists: they printed to the wrong printer, they're at the wrong system's machine, or the job never left their laptop.

Their PIN is in the **member app → More → Printing**, in large type. Point them there rather than reading it out — they'll need it again.

## Paper

4. Check and refill trays on the morning walk.
5. Load with the correct orientation and don't overfill past the line — overfilling is the main cause of misfeeds.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** paper sizes stocked (A4 only? A3?), where paper is kept, and reorder point.

## Toner

6. Replace when the panel says low, not when it stops. A machine that dies mid-function booking is a bad look.
7. Keep at least one spare of each colour.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** toner cartridge codes for each machine, supplier, and where spares live.

## Jams

8. Follow the **panel's animation** — it shows which door and which roller.
9. Pull jammed paper **in the direction of travel**, slowly, and get every fragment. A torn scrap left behind causes the next jam.
10. Close all doors firmly; the machine won't resume with one ajar.
11. If it jams again in the same place twice, stop and log it — that's a roller or a sensor.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending a member to the wrong floor's machine.** Check their floor first.
- **Yanking jammed paper backwards.** Tears it and leaves fragments.
- **Waiting for toner to run out.** Replace on the warning.
- **Raising a print charge by hand.** Usage syncs from PaperCut into fees and bills itself — a manual fee double-charges. See [Add a fee or charge](../billing/add-a-recurring-fee.md).
- **Clearing a jam and not checking the output.** The pages before the jam are often creased or missing.

## If something goes wrong

- **Repeated jams** — log under Maintenance with the machine and the location in the paper path.
- **Error code on the panel** — write the exact code before clearing it; it's the first thing the technician asks.
- **A member's job vanished** — jobs expire after a period. They reprint. Nothing is recoverable at the machine.
- **A machine down and someone needs to print now** — send them to the other floor's machine, but they'll need that system's PIN.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** service contact for each machine, and whether the building or a supplier handles faults.
