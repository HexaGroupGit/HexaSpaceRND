## Purpose

Get a room's display, sound and camera working when a meeting is about to start.

## When to do this

A member can't get their meeting going, or you're setting a room up ahead of a booking.

## The rooms

Meeting rooms: **Sky**, **Earth**, **Sun**, **Moon**. Divisible space: **North**, **South**, **West**, which together are the **Function Space**. Plus **Media Studios** and the **Podcast Room**.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** what AV is actually in each room — display size, ClickShare, camera, speakers, mics, HDMI, room speakerphone. They're clearly not all the same, and knowing which room has what is most of the job.

## Order to try things

**Always fall back to cable before debugging wireless.** A meeting waiting is worth more than a tidy solution.

1. **Display on** and on the right input.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** are displays left on standby, and is there a remote or a wall panel?
2. **Wireless share** — see [ClickShare](clickshare.md).
3. **HDMI cable** — the fallback. Win + P → Duplicate on Windows; on Mac it usually just appears.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** is there an HDMI cable in every room, and which adapters (USB-C, Mini DisplayPort) are kept at reception?
4. **Sound** — check it's set to the room's output on the laptop, not the laptop speakers. This is the single most common "the AV is broken".
5. **Camera and mic** for a video call — selected in Zoom/Teams settings, not just plugged in.

## Setting a room up ahead of a booking

- Check today's **Bookings** for what's coming and how many people.
- Screen clear, cables coiled on the table, whiteboard wiped, markers that work.
- For a **function**, remember the 30-minute buffer each side — the room is blocked longer than the event.
- For a **Function Space** booking, North/South/West are all in use — see [Why booking one Function Space room blocks the others](../bookings/function-room-conflicts.md).

## Common mistakes

- **Debugging wireless with a room full of people.** Cable first.
- **Fixing the picture and forgetting the sound.** Check both before you walk away.
- **Assuming the member's laptop is the problem.** Try a known-good device before declaring it theirs.
- **Not logging a recurring fault.** A room that fails weekly needs a fix, not a daily rescue.
- **Leaving adapters in rooms.** They disappear. Back to reception.

## If something goes wrong

- **No picture on any input** — display or cable. Log it and move the meeting to another room.
- **Persistent dropouts** — likely Wi-Fi or a failing cable; log it as a pattern with the room name.
- **Meeting starting now and the room won't work** — move them. There's usually a free room; check Bookings.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** who fixes AV here — in-house, the building, or a contractor? And is there a spare display or a loan kit for a function that fails?
