## Purpose

Keep the shared kitchen and lounge in the state members were promised — the space they see most and judge us on.

## When to do this

Morning, a midday reset, and end of day.

## What members are promised

Their welcome pack commits us to **barista-style coffee, tea and filtered water in the lounge**. That's the standard to hold.

## Morning

1. Benches clear and wiped.
2. Dishwasher emptied from overnight.
3. Sink empty — nothing soaking.
4. Filtered water working and clean.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** filtered water — tap, dispenser or bottled? Filter change interval?
5. Tea, sugar, cups, spoons stocked.
6. Fridge — milk in date, nothing growing.
7. Tables and lounge seating straightened, rubbish cleared.
8. Coffee machine — see [Coffee machine — daily](coffee-machine-daily.md).

## Midday reset

The kitchen is worst around 1–2pm. A ten-minute pass then is worth more than an hour at 5pm.

9. Clear and wipe benches, run the dishwasher, empty bins if full, restock cups.

## End of day

10. Everything into the dishwasher and run it.
11. Benches, sink and tables wiped down.
12. Bins out.
13. Fridge — clear anything obviously abandoned.
    > **NEEDS INPUT:** fridge clear-out policy. Weekly? Is anything labelled and left, or is it all binned?

## Member-owned items

> **NEEDS INPUT:** how is member food handled? Is there labelled fridge space, and what's the rule on how long things stay? This is the most common source of friction in a shared kitchen and worth being explicit about.

The House Rules attached to every contract are the backstop for anything contested — check what they already say before inventing a rule.

## Common mistakes

- **Only cleaning at end of day.** The lounge looks worst at lunchtime, when the most people see it.
- **Leaving the dishwasher full and unrun.** People then leave dishes in the sink.
- **Restocking cups but not tea.** Check the whole set.
- **Throwing out labelled member food** without a stated policy.
- **Treating it as the cleaners' job.** Cleaners do a pass; the state through the day is ours.

## If something goes wrong

- **Fridge not cold, dishwasher not draining, tap leaking** — log under Maintenance and take it out of use.
- **Persistent mess from one suite** — that's a conversation with that member, not a bigger bin.
- **Something spoiled or a smell** — deal with it immediately; members will not report it, they'll just think less of the space.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** cleaner schedule — which days, what they cover, and what's explicitly left to staff. Half the value of this SOP is the boundary between the two.
