## Purpose

Get the floor ready before the first member arrives, and catch anything that broke overnight while there's still time to fix it.

## When to do this

Every morning, before opening.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** opening time, and who opens on weekdays vs weekends.

## Before you start

Members have 24/7 access, so someone may already be in the building. Opening is about getting the space *presentable and working*, not about unlocking it.

## Steps — the floor

1. **Lights and climate** on across the floor.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** where the lighting and HVAC controls are, and any overnight setback to reverse.
2. **Walk the floor.** Lounge, kitchen, meeting rooms, end-of-trip facilities, corridors. You're looking for: mess left overnight, furniture out of place, anything broken, anything that smells.
3. **Reset meeting rooms** — chairs in, tables clear, whiteboards wiped, cables coiled and on the table not the floor.
4. **Check today's bookings** in the admin app under **Bookings** or **Calendar**, so you know which rooms need to be right and when.

## Steps — kitchen and coffee

5. Run the **coffee machine morning routine** — see [Coffee machine — daily](coffee-machine-daily.md).
6. Check consumables: coffee beans, milk, tea, filtered water, cups. See [Consumables and restocking](consumables-restocking.md).
7. Empty anything left in the sink or dishwasher from overnight.

## Steps — systems

8. **Open the admin app Dashboard.** This is not optional housekeeping — parts of offboarding only run when an admin loads the app. See [Daily opening routine](../start-here/daily-opening-routine.md) for what to action.
9. Check the **lobby directory screens** on Levels 4 and 2 are showing and not frozen — see [Update the lobby directory screens](../front-of-house/update-directory-screens.md).
10. Check the printers have paper and no error on the panel.
11. Check the Wi-Fi is up — the **Hexa Spaces** network should be visible and connecting.

## Steps — front of house

12. Reception desk clear and stocked.
13. Check **Mail & Deliveries** for anything logged yesterday and not collected.
14. Check **Messages** and **Maintenance** for anything raised overnight.

## What to do if something's wrong

| Found | Do |
|---|---|
| Something broken | Log it under **Maintenance** — even if you're fixing it yourself |
| Mess beyond a quick tidy | Log it, and note which space; repeated mess in one suite is a conversation with that member |
| A member locked out | See [Incidents and lockouts](incidents-and-lockouts.md) |
| Coffee machine won't run | [Coffee machine — daily](coffee-machine-daily.md), then escalate |
| Directory screen blank | Open the plain-HTML link on a computer first — if that renders, reboot the TV |

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the Dashboard** because nothing looks urgent. Loading it is what runs pending offboarding work.
- **Fixing without logging.** Three reports of the same fault look like one-off grumbles if nobody logs them.
- **Resetting rooms after members arrive.** The point is to be ahead of the first booking.
- **Assuming overnight was quiet** because the floor looks fine. Check Messages and Maintenance.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** anything specific to this building — alarm or security panel, loading dock, bin day, blinds, music, front-door signage, and whether Levels 2 and 5 are opened by the same person or separately.
