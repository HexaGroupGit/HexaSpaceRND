## Purpose

Never run out of the things members notice immediately — coffee, milk, paper, toner, toilet supplies.

## When to do this

Check daily on the opening walk. Order on a fixed day so it becomes routine rather than a scramble.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** which day orders go in, and who places them.

## What to check daily

| Area | Items |
|---|---|
| **Coffee** | Beans, milk, alternative milks, sugar, cups, lids, takeaway cups |
| **Kitchen** | Tea, dishwasher tablets, detergent, cloths, bin liners, paper towel |
| **Print** | Paper (both floors), toner (each colour, each machine) |
| **Bathrooms / end-of-trip** | Toilet paper, hand soap, hand towel, shower supplies |
| **Meeting rooms** | Whiteboard markers that actually work, erasers, notepads, pens |
| **Reception** | Visitor supplies, stationery |

> **NEEDS INPUT:** suppliers and account details for each category, typical lead time, and the reorder point for the big ones (beans, milk, toner, paper).

## The rule that prevents most stockouts

**Reorder at one spare, not at zero.** Anything with a lead time longer than a day — toner especially — should trigger an order when the last spare is opened, not when it runs out.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** is there a storeroom? Where, and what's kept there?

## Steps

1. On the opening walk, check each area above.
2. Restock from the store to the floor.
3. Note anything that hit its reorder point.
4. Place the order on the ordering day, or immediately if it's coffee, milk or toner.
5. Put deliveries away the day they arrive — a box in the corridor is both a trip hazard and a way to lose stock.

## Members' expectations

The welcome pack promises **barista-style coffee, tea and filtered water**, plus **end-of-trip facilities**. Those are contractual-adjacent commitments, not nice-to-haves — an empty bean hopper or an out-of-soap shower is a promise broken.

## Common mistakes

- **Checking only what you can see.** Look in the cupboard, not just at the bench.
- **Ordering when it runs out.** Toner and beans both have lead times.
- **Restocking the floor and not the store**, so the next person finds an empty cupboard.
- **Not tracking alternative milks.** They run out faster than dairy and people notice.
- **Leaving deliveries boxed in a corridor.**

## If something goes wrong

- **Out of coffee mid-week** — buy retail locally today and fix the ordering. Members should not experience the gap.
- **A delivery is short or wrong** — check off against the order at delivery, not later. Raise it the same day.
- **A supplier is repeatedly late** — that's worth changing, not working around.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** budget or spend limit staff can commit without approval. Without this, people either overspend or leave the space short because they didn't want to ask.
