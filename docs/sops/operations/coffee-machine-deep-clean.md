## Purpose

The periodic clean that the daily routine doesn't cover — the one that stops a slow decline nobody notices until the coffee is bad and the machine is expensive.

## When to do this

> **NEEDS INPUT:** frequency. Typically weekly for a full backflush and group-head strip, monthly-to-quarterly for descaling depending on water hardness and volume. What's the schedule here, and does the machine prompt for it?

Do it when the space is quiet — **NEEDS INPUT:** which day/time.

## Before you start

- The machine will be **out of service** for the duration. Put a sign up; members will otherwise queue.
- Have the right products. **Descaler and backflush detergent are different chemicals** — using one for the other either does nothing or damages the machine.

> **NEEDS INPUT:** which products are used, and where they're kept.

## Steps — deep clean

1. **Backflush with detergent** through the blind filter, repeated cycles, then repeat with water only until it runs clear. Any detergent left behind will taste.
2. **Strip the group head** — remove the shower screen and gasket, soak in cleaning solution, brush the seat.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** does this machine allow it without tools, or does it need a service?
3. **Portafilters and baskets** — soak in the same solution, scrub, rinse thoroughly.
4. **Grinder** — empty the hopper, wipe out the oils, brush out the chute and around the burrs. Coffee oil goes rancid and is a common cause of "the beans taste off".
5. **Milk system** — full clean cycle with milk-system cleaner, then rinse until it runs clear.
6. **Drip tray, grounds bin, panels** — out, washed, dried.
7. **Water filter** — check and replace if due.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** is there an inline water filter? Type and replacement interval.

## Steps — descale

Only when due, and only with actual descaler.

8. Follow the machine's descale cycle.
   > **NEEDS INPUT:** does it have an automatic descale program, or is it manual?
9. **Rinse thoroughly afterwards.** Descaler residue tastes appalling and members will tell you.
10. Run and discard the first few shots and a milk steam before serving.

## After

11. Pull a test shot and taste it. If it tastes of chemicals, rinse again.
12. Note the date it was done.
    > **NEEDS INPUT:** where is this recorded? A log by the machine, or a Maintenance entry? Recording it is what makes the schedule real rather than aspirational.

## Common mistakes

- **Descaling instead of backflushing**, or the reverse. Different problems, different chemicals.
- **Not rinsing enough.** The most common complaint after a deep clean is chemical taste.
- **Doing it at 9am.** The machine is out for a while.
- **Skipping the grinder.** People clean the machine and ignore the grinder, which is where stale oil lives.
- **Not recording it.** Without a record, nobody knows whether it's been six days or six weeks.

## If something goes wrong

- **It won't come back up to pressure** — reseat the shower screen and gasket; a misaligned gasket is the usual cause.
- **Water where it shouldn't be** — off at the wall, log under Maintenance, escalate.
- **Still tastes wrong after rinsing** — rinse more, then check the water filter.
- **Anything you had to force** — stop, and get the technician. A stripped fitting costs more than a service call.
