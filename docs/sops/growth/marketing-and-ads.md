## Purpose

Promote vacant space and generate enquiries.

## When to do this

Vacancy is rising, or a campaign needs building or reviewing.

## The two tabs

**Marketing** opens with a count of how many spaces are currently vacant to promote, and splits into:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Ads** | Build and run campaigns, push to Google Ads, review reporting |
| **AI Studio** | Generate campaign content and creative |

Leads and enquiries live in **CRM**, not here. Event registrations live under **Events**.

## Steps

1. Open **Marketing** and check the vacancy count in the header — that's what you're promoting.
2. Work in **Ads** to build the campaign.
3. Use **AI Studio** to draft copy and creative.
4. Push to Google Ads once connected in Settings → Integrations.
5. Review reporting against enquiries arriving in the CRM.

## What happens automatically

- The vacancy count is live from the spaces inventory.
- Enquiries generated by campaigns arrive through the normal website path and land in **CRM → Enquiries** — see [How website enquiries arrive](website-enquiries.md).
- A visitor arriving via a referral link carries that attribution into the lead.

## Common mistakes

- **Advertising a space that's reserved.** Check the floor plan — reserved means someone is part-way through taking it.
- **Promoting a rate that hasn't been approved.** An advertised price is a public commitment.
- **Looking for leads under Marketing.** They're in CRM.
- **Publishing AI-drafted copy unread.** It's a draft, not a decision.
- **Leaving a campaign running against filled space.** Cross-check the vacancy count.

## If something goes wrong

- **Ads aren't pushing** — check the Google Ads connection in Settings → Integrations.
- **Enquiries aren't arriving** — test the website form; the path runs through the public enquiry endpoint.
- **A listing shows a leased space** — space listings re-sync when a unit is leased, but check the published listing.
